OFFICIAL NAME:
TACSFON (OAUSTECH)

FULL NAME:
The Apostolic Church Student Fellowship of Nigeria

LOCATION:
OAUSTECH, Okitipupa, Ondo State, Nigeria

ABOUT THE FELLOWSHIP:

TACSFON (OAUSTECH) is a Christian student fellowship within the
Olusegun Agagu University of Science and Technology (OAUSTECH),
Okitipupa, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The fellowship is a student-led expression of The Apostolic Church
(TAC) within the university environment. It is made up of students
who worship and serve within The Apostolic Church, although
membership in the fellowship is not restricted only to students who
are members of TAC from home.

TACSFON exists to provide a spiritual community where students can
grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ, fellowship with other
believers, study the Word of God, pray, worship, evangelize, and
serve God while navigating university life.

The fellowship is governed and operated by students of OAUSTECH,
with its activities and organization shaped around the needs and
spiritual growth of students.

IDENTITY / POSITIONING:

TACSFON should not be presented as being in competition with other
Christian student fellowships or churches.

Other Christian fellowships such as RCCG, Winners, and others should
be treated respectfully. The website should communicate that
different Christian fellowships may have different structures,
denominations, and expressions of faith, while sharing the common
purpose of serving Christ.

TACSFON's distinctive identity comes from its connection to
The Apostolic Church and its expression as a student fellowship
within OAUSTECH.

CORE MESSAGE:

TACSFON is a place where students can encounter Christ, grow in
faith, build meaningful Christian fellowship, and serve God within
their university community.

The tone should communicate:

- faith
- warmth
- fellowship
- service
- spiritual growth
- community
- purpose
- student life
- devotion to Christ

Do not make the organization sound unnecessarily corporate.

Do not make the copy sound like an advertisement.

The writing should feel like a genuine Christian fellowship speaking
to students, visitors, prospective members, and members of the
broader TAC community.

9. FELLOWSHIP ACTIVITIES & PROGRAMS
============================================================

TACSFON's activities are centered around worship, the study of God's
Word, prayer, spiritual growth, evangelism, fellowship, and service.

The website should distinguish between regular weekly activities,
periodic activities, and major annual programs.

============================================================
9.1 REGULAR WEEKLY ACTIVITIES
============================================================

SUNDAY SERVICE

TACSFON gathers for Sunday worship and fellowship.

This is one of the primary gatherings of the fellowship and should
be presented as a time for students to worship God, hear His Word,
pray, and fellowship with one another.

TUESDAY — BIBLE STUDY

Bible Study holds on Tuesdays.

The Bible Study is focused on studying and understanding Scripture,
growing in biblical knowledge, and applying God's Word to everyday
Christian life.

The presentation should communicate that Bible Study is an
opportunity to ask questions, learn, reflect, and grow together.

FRIDAY — PRAYER MEETING

Prayer Meeting holds on Fridays.

Prayer is an important part of the fellowship's life.

The prayer gathering should be presented as a time for students to
seek God together, intercede, give thanks, and grow in their personal
and collective prayer lives.

SATURDAY — CHOIR PRACTICE

Choir Practice holds on Saturdays.

The Choir/Choir Unit prepares through practice and musical
rehearsals for worship and fellowship activities.

The website should present this as part of the fellowship's service
and worship structure rather than simply as a music club.

============================================================
9.2 MONTHLY / PERIODIC ACTIVITIES
============================================================

VIGIL

A vigil is held once a month, on a Friday.

The vigil should be presented as a dedicated time of extended
prayer, worship, reflection, and seeking God.

The exact date of each vigil may vary.

Therefore, do not hardcode a recurring calendar date.

When a specific vigil is scheduled, it may be displayed through the
dynamic Events system.

============================================================
9.3 EVANGELISM
============================================================

TACSFON engages in evangelism periodically.

These activities are focused on sharing the gospel and reaching
people with the message of Christ.

Evangelism should be presented as an active expression of the
fellowship's Christian faith and commitment to sharing Christ beyond
the fellowship community.

Evangelism activities may occur at different times and should
therefore be represented as events/programs whenever a specific
activity is scheduled.

============================================================
9.4 ANNUAL PROGRAMS
============================================================

Some TACSFON activities occur annually rather than weekly or
monthly.

One major example is:

TACSFON WEEK / ANNIVERSARY

TACSFON Week is an annual period of special programs celebrating and
reflecting on the fellowship.

It may contain multiple activities and programs across several days.

Because the exact structure may change from year to year, the website
should not hardcode a fixed list of activities for TACSFON Week.

Instead, individual programs can be represented as dynamic events
when the relevant information is available.

The annual program may include special worship gatherings, teachings,
outreach, celebrations, or other fellowship activities depending on
the year's schedule.

Do not invent specific TACSFON Week activities unless they are
provided.

============================================================

TACSFON (OAUSTECH) is part of the Joint Campus Christian Fellowship
(JCCF).

The website should acknowledge this relationship as part of TACSFON's

campus.

Do not create unsupported claims about JCCF's organizational
structure, leadership, history, membership, or activities.

If additional official JCCF information is provided later, it can be
incorporated into the website.

UNITS
==================================================

SECTION PURPOSE:
TACSFON (OAUSTECH) operates through different units that help coordinate the
spiritual, academic, administrative, creative, and fellowship activities of
the organization.

Each unit has a coordinator/head who helps lead and coordinate the activities
of the unit.

The Units section should communicate that every unit is an area of service
within one fellowship. The presentation should feel collaborative and
community-oriented, not like a corporate departmental structure.

--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for coordinating and facilitating the fellowship's Bible study
activities.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Coordinates the Tuesday Bible Study.

- Helps prepare and organize Bible study sessions.
- Encourages members to study and understand God's Word.

REGULAR ACTIVITY:
- Tuesday Bible Study


UNIT: PRAYER UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for coordinating the fellowship's prayer activities.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Coordinates the Friday Prayer Meeting.

- Helps organize prayer-focused activities and gatherings.
- Supports the fellowship's culture of prayer.

REGULAR ACTIVITY:
- Friday Prayer Meeting


UNIT: DRAMA UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for coordinating drama presentations within the fellowship.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Coordinates drama presentations during fellowship programmes and events.
- Organizes and prepares members involved in drama plays.
- Works with other units when drama presentations are required.
- Coordinates drama activities for special programmes and events.


UNIT: CHOIR UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for the fellowship's choir, musical ministrations, and choir

RESPONSIBILITIES:

- Conducts regular choir practice.
- Prepares members for musical ministrations and special programmes.

REGULAR ACTIVITY:
- Saturday Choir Practice


UNIT: ORGANIZING UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for helping coordinate the practical and logistical arrangements
required for fellowship activities and events.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Organizes and packs instruments and other equipment.
- Coordinates transportation for fellowship members attending events.
- Helps organize logistics for programmes and activities.
- Works with other units to ensure events and programmes run smoothly.
- Handles practical arrangements required before, during, and after events.


UNIT: ACADEMIC UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Supports members in their academic pursuits alongside their spiritual and
fellowship life.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Organizes tutorials and academic assistance.
- Coordinates academic-focused programmes.
- Supports academic Sunday activities.
- Creates opportunities for members to assist and learn from one another
  academically.

NOTE:
The unit should be presented as part of the fellowship's student-focused
community and its desire to support members throughout their university
journey.


UNIT: USHERING UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Helps maintain order, hospitality, and smooth coordination during fellowship
services and programmes.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Ushers members during fellowship services.
- Supports the orderly flow of weekly fellowship services.
- Assists with seating, movement, and general service coordination where
  necessary.
- Supports ushering responsibilities during special programmes and events.

NOTE:
Membership/participation in the Ushering Unit is not compulsory for every
fellowship member.


UNIT: EVANGELISM UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
Responsible for coordinating evangelism and outreach activities.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Anchors evangelism activities.
- Coordinates outreach and reach-out programmes.
- Encourages members to participate in sharing the Gospel.
- Supports periodic evangelism activities organized by the fellowship.


UNIT: BROTHERS UNIT
--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE:
The Brothers Unit brings together the brothers within the fellowship for
fellowship, connection, brotherhood, and shared activities.

ACTIVITIES:
- May organize fellowship activities such as football events and other

- Activities may vary depending on the plans and needs of the fellowship.

NOTE:
Do not present the examples above as an exhaustive or permanent list of
activities.

PURPOSE:
The Sisters Unit brings together the sisters within the fellowship for

ACTIVITIES:

Coordinates the activities of the Ushering Unit.

services and other programmes.


ORGANIZING COORDINATOR:
Coordinates the activities of the Organizing Unit.

The coordinator helps oversee practical and logistical arrangements for
fellowship activities and events.


ACADEMIC COORDINATOR:
Coordinates the activities of the Academic Unit.

The coordinator helps organize academic assistance, tutorials, academic
programmes, and other activities designed to support members academically.


DRAMA COORDINATORS:
There are two Drama Coordinators responsible for coordinating the Drama Unit.

The coordinators work together to organize drama presentations, prepare

Coordinates the technical aspects required for fellowship programmes and

============================================================
2. ACADEMIC HUB PURPOSE
============================================================

Academic Hub exists to give students organized access to academic
materials for their current academic semester.

The platform should make it easier for students to discover and
study from materials that may otherwise be scattered across
different files, devices, chats, and sources.

The platform may contain:

- Past Questions
- Manuals / Lecture Notes
- Workbooks
- Materials

A student's active semester subscription gives them access to all
published Academic Hub materials available for that semester.

Do not claim that a particular course, department, school, or
material exists unless it has actually been added to the system.

9. SEMESTER ACCESS
============================================================

A student's Academic Hub subscription applies to a specific
semester.

The student receives access to all published Academic Hub materials
available for that semester.

Access begins according to the configured semester/access rules and
expires at the configured semester end date.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT calculate access as a fixed number of weeks from the date of
payment.

For example, if a semester runs for approximately 13 weeks and a
student subscribes during week 8, the student's access should still
end when the configured semester ends.

The semester dates are authoritative.

A student's access should therefore follow:

    Semester Start
            ↓
    Semester End

rather than:

    Payment Date + 13 Weeks

21. USER ACCESS / ACCOUNT SHARING
============================================================

Academic Hub access should be tied to the student's individual
account.

Users should not receive permanent public URLs to protected
materials.

Protected resources should require:

- Authentication
- Valid semester entitlement
- Server-side authorization

The system should include reasonable protections against casual
account sharing.

Where appropriate, session/device limitations may be used.

Do not create unnecessarily restrictive access rules that make
legitimate student usage difficult.


============================================================
22. ACCESS EXPIRATION
============================================================

When the student's semester entitlement expires:

- Protected materials become inaccessible.
- The account remains available.
- Payment history remains available.
- Historical semester information remains preserved.
- The student may subscribe to a future active semester.

Do not delete expired entitlements.
